Algorithm: Maximum and Minimum of Three Numbers

1. Start
2. Declare variables A, B, C, MAX and MIN.
3. Read A, B and C.
4. Compare A, B and C to find the largest number.
5. Store the largest number in MAX.
6. Compare A, B and C to find the smallest number.
7. Store the smallest number in MIN.
8. Display MAX and MIN.
9. Stop
